Who does peatland R&I, and with whom?

Turverkko documents public research and innovation funding for peatlands, peat fields, peatland forests, paludiculture and restoration, as well as soil emissions, carbon sequestration and their measurement (MRV), in six EU peatland regions. It shows where the same work is being done in parallel without a connection.

Method and limitations

        
The data is a sample verified against sources, not the full population. Projects were collected from national registers, EU CAP Network project pages, programme announcements and research portals. Every link has a source and an evidence class: source, inferred or unverified.

        
The collaboration network includes participation, membership of a centre, documented collaboration and organisations' co-publications. Similar projects without a link are project pairs whose texts are similar but which share no actor and have no collaboration path within three steps. This means no link was found in public sources; it does not prove there is none. Hectares have been checked by hand, with quotations.
